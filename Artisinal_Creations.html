Lobster Spheres With Chocolate Soil

A bold exercise in molecular gastronomy and unexpected flavour pairing, this avant-garde canapé combines the luxury of lobster with the earthy richness of chocolate. Delicate lobster mince is transformed into glossy, caviar-like spheres using sodium alginate spherification, then nestled on a bed of nutty chocolate soil. The result is a visually arresting, texturally dynamic bite that challenges convention - sweet meets savoury, land meets sea - while showcasing advanced culinary technique and creative confidence.

Components & Additional Information

Components

  • Lobster Mince Spheres (Sodium Alginate): Fresh lobster meat finely minced and blended into a smooth purée, then mixed with sodium alginate. The mixture is carefully dropped into a calcium chloride bath, where spherification occurs - creating delicate, translucent spheres with a thin gel membrane that bursts gently on the palate, releasing the sweet, briny essence of lobster. The spheres are rinsed and held in a neutral liquid to maintain their shape and glossy appearance.
  • Nutty Chocolate Soil: A crumbly, earthy base made from dark chocolate, ground nuts (likely hazelnuts or almonds), and a binding agent such as cocoa powder or breadcrumbs, dehydrated to achieve a dry, soil-like texture. The chocolate provides bittersweet depth, while the nuts add toasted richness and crunch. The soil serves as both flavour foundation and visual contrast - dark, matte, and rustic against the glossy, pale spheres.

Additional Information

Demonstrates advanced molecular gastronomy (sodium alginate spherification), lobster butchery and purée preparation, chocolate and nut soil creation, and precise plating for volume service. The dish showcases technical mastery, creative risk-taking, and the ability to execute complex techniques consistently under pressure - essential for high-end events, tasting menus, and culinary competitions. A conversation-starter canapé that demonstrates innovation, technical skill, and the confidence to challenge traditional flavour boundaries. Perfect for clients seeking cutting-edge, memorable culinary experiences.

Chicken Lollipops With Coconut Peppadew Sauce

A vibrant, crowd-pleasing dish that marries crispy, golden chicken lollipops with a bold, creamy coconut Peppadew sauce. The chicken is expertly frenched - meat pushed down the drumette to create a lollipop handle - then marinated, fried to a perfect crunch, and glazed with a sauce that balances sweet coconut richness with the tangy, piquant heat of Peppadew peppers. This dish is interactive, visually striking, and designed for easy service at events, buffets, and cocktail receptions.

Components & Additional Information

Components

  • Chicken Lollipops: Chicken drumettes frenched to expose the bone, creating a clean handle for easy eating. Marinated in a blend of spices (likely garlic, ginger, paprika, and aromatic herbs), then coated in seasoned breadcrumbs and deep-fried until golden and crispy. The meat remains juicy inside while the exterior delivers a satisfying crunch.
  • Coconut Peppadew Sauce: A creamy, vibrant sauce that combines the sweetness of coconut milk or cream with the tangy, mildly spicy kick of Peppadew peppers. The sauce is blended smooth, then reduced to a glossy, pourable consistency. It clings beautifully to the chicken, adding colour, moisture, and a bold flavour profile that's both comforting and exciting.
  • Garnish: Fresh coriander (cilantro) leaves scattered over the top add a bright, herbal note and a pop of green that contrasts the golden sauce and crispy chicken.

Additional Information

Preparation: Demonstrates knife skills (frenching the drumettes), marination for flavour penetration, frying technique for even colour and crispness, and sauce emulsification. The dish is designed for volume production while maintaining quality and visual appeal—essential for catering and event work. A versatile crowd-pleaser that scales well and travels confidently.

Pairing: Serve with cooling cucumber raita or lime crema for contrast.

Origin: Inspired by French pâtisserie techniques with modern plating style.

Beef Fillet With Potato Coulis And Onion Purée Quenelles, Dark Chocolate Jus

A sophisticated fine-dining main that marries classic French technique with unexpected depth and elegance. Perfectly seared beef fillet, cooked to a tender medium-rare, is accompanied by silky potato coulis, vibrant green onion purée quenelles, and a rich dark chocolate jus that adds complexity and luxurious body. The dish is a study in balance - earthy, sweet, savoury, and subtly bitter-plated with modern restraint and artistic confidence.

Components & Additional Information

Components

  • Beef Fillet: Premium centre-cut beef fillet, seasoned simply with salt and pepper, then seared in a hot pan to develop a caramelised crust while maintaining a rosy, tender interior. Cooked to medium-rare for optimal texture and flavour, the beef is rested before slicing to reveal its perfect doneness. The meat is the star - rich, buttery, and deeply savoury.
  • Potato Coulis: A smooth, velvety purée made from high-quality potatoes, cream, butter, and seasoning, blended to a pourable consistency. The coulis is lighter than traditional mashed potato, offering creamy comfort without heaviness. It provides a neutral, starchy base that anchors the dish and balances the richness of the beef and jus.
  • Onion Purée Quenelles: Caramelised onions blended to a silky purée, enriched with cream, and tinted vibrant green - likely with spinach, herbs (parsley & basil) for colour. The purée is shaped into elegant quenelles using two spoons, creating smooth, oval forms that add visual sophistication and textural variety. The onion provides sweetness and depth, while the green hue adds freshness and visual contrast.
  • Dark Chocolate Jus: A refined, glossy sauce made from a rich beef or veal reduction, finished with a touch of dark chocolate to add complexity, subtle bitterness, and a luxurious, velvety mouthfeel. The chocolate deepens the jus without sweetness, enhancing the umami and creating a sophisticated, unexpected flavour layer that elevates the dish beyond traditional beef sauces.

Additional Information

Demonstrates butchery and meat cookery (searing and resting beef to perfect doneness), purée and coulis preparation (achieving silky, pourable textures), quenelle shaping (precision and elegance), and sauce reduction with chocolate integration. The dish showcases advanced fine-dining technique, flavour layering, and the ability to balance bold, unexpected elements with classical foundations. The plating is confident, minimalist, and designed to showcase each component's individual beauty while maintaining visual harmony.

Pistachio-Coated Lamb Chops With Lentil Curry Bedding

An elegant fusion of French technique and bold South Asian flavour, this dish showcases tender lamb chops encrusted with vibrant green pistachios, resting on a richly spiced lentil curry base. The pistachio crust adds nutty sweetness and textural contrast to the succulent lamb, while the lentil curry provides earthy depth and aromatic warmth. It's a sophisticated main course that balances refinement with robust, comforting flavours.

Components & Additional Information

Components

  • Lamb Chops:Premium lamb cutlets, French-trimmed to expose clean bones for elegant presentation. Seasoned and seared to achieve a golden crust, then cooked to medium-rare for optimal tenderness and flavour. The meat remains juicy and pink inside, with a caramelised exterior that locks in richness.
  • Pistachio Crust:Finely crushed pistachios mixed with breadcrumbs, herbs (likely parsley or mint), and a binding agent (Dijon mustard or egg wash). The crust is pressed onto the lamb after searing, then finished in the oven to achieve a golden, nutty coating that adds visual vibrancy, textural crunch, and a subtle sweetness that complements the lamb's natural richness.
  • Lentil Curry Bedding:A fragrant, spiced lentil base cooked with aromatics, tomatoes, and a blend of warming spices. The lentils are simmered to a creamy, slightly loose consistency that spreads beautifully across the plate, providing both flavour foundation and visual contrast to the lamb. The curry is earthy, mildly spiced, and rich enough to stand alongside the lamb without overpowering it.
  • GarnishFresh herbs (coriander or parsley) scattered over the top add brightness and a pop of green that echoes the pistachio crust.

Additional Information

Demonstrates butchery skills (French-trimming lamb), searing and roasting technique for even cooking, crust application and finishing, and curry preparation with balanced spicing. The dish showcases the ability to marry European and South Asian culinary traditions with confidence and finesse—essential for modern fine dining and diverse event menus.

Lemon Meringue Tartlet With Dehydrated Orange Wheel

A refined interpretation of the classic lemon meringue pie, reimagined as an individual tartlet that balances sharp citrus brightness with pillowy sweetness. Silky lemon curd fills a crisp pastry shell, crowned with torched Italian meringue and finished with a striking dehydrated orange wheel.

Components & Additional Information

Components

  • Shortcrust Tartlet Shell: Golden, buttery pastry blind-baked to achieve a delicate snap and neutral canvas that allows the lemon curd's acidity to shine.
  • Lemon Curd Filling: Vibrant, glossy curd made from fresh lemon juice, zest, eggs, butter, and sugar. Intensely citrus-forward yet balanced—sharp enough to awaken the palate, rich enough to satisfy. Set to a soft, luxurious texture.
  • Torched Italian Meringue: Stable, marshmallow-like meringue piped in elegant swirls and caramelised to golden perfection. Adds architectural height, textural contrast, and toasted sweetness that tempers the curd's acidity.
  • Dehydrated Orange Wheel: Translucent citrus disc, slowly dried to concentrate natural sugars and essential oils. Serves as both striking visual centrepiece and flavour bridge—bittersweet and aromatic, complementing the lemon while adding sophistication.
  • Plating Accents: Cinnamon dust, fresh microgreens (lemon thyme or micro mint), torched meringue kisses, and lemon curd gel dots create rhythm and movement across the matte turquoise plate.

Additional Information

Technique Highlights: Demonstrates mastery across pastry, curd preparation, Italian meringue work, dehydration, and plating. The asymmetrical composition is clean, confident, and repeatable—essential for volume service. Background plating confirms consistency under pressure.
