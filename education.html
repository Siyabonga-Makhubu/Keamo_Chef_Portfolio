SAMSA SRC / VHF Radio

The SAMSA VHF Radio Course is a short maritime communications training program designed to teach candidates how to safely and correctly operate a marine VHF radio onboard vessels. It is typically a 1-day course and leads to the Short Range Certificate (SRC), which is the minimum legal requirement for operating VHF radio equipment in South Africa and many maritime regions.

L2 Wine Appreciation

The Level 2 Wine Appreciation Course is an introductory to intermediate program designed to build foundational knowledge of wine, including grape varieties, wine styles, tasting techniques, and food pairing principles. It is ideal for beginners in the hospitality industry, wine enthusiasts, and anyone looking to expand their understanding of wine.
