About Me

Chef | EET SUM AFRIKA

I'm Keamogetswe Moagi — also known as Chef K — a South African chef and culinary entrepreneur based in Gauteng. I specialize in African-inspired cuisine with a fine dining approach, blending bold local flavours with international technique. Through my brands EET SUM AFRIKA (catering) and SIP SUM AFRIKA (beverages), I create elevated culinary experiences for private dinners, exclusive events, and bespoke gatherings.

My approach to cooking is rooted in a deep respect for fresh, seasonal, and locally sourced ingredients, combined with the rich traditions of modern South African cuisine. I aim to tell a story through every plate by merging heritage with contemporary presentation, ensuring each dish is both visually striking and full of meaning.

I thrive in high-pressure, detail-driven environments where precision, timing, and creativity are essential. Whether I’m leading a private dining experience or executing an intimate event, I remain hands-on, adaptable, and focused on delivering seamless hospitality. I pride myself on anticipating needs, refining flavours in real time, and maintaining consistency in every service.

I am passionate about inclusivity at the table and confidently cater to a wide range of dietary requirements, including kosher, vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten-free, keto, banting, and halaal diets. My goal is always to ensure that every guest enjoys a memorable dining experience without compromise.

Beyond private dining, I also offer catering for events, corporate functions, and lifestyle experiences, as well as culinary consulting for kitchen operations and menu development. I am fluent in English and Setswana, adaptable in international environments, and comfortable working in diverse settings including yacht galleys and remote service locations.

I am available for private catering, events, yacht opportunities, and culinary collaborations where bold flavours, refined technique, and exceptional hospitality are the standard.

Amuse-Bouche

Milk tart Pops with Chocolate Soil

Milk Tart Pops with Chocolate Soil is an elegant reimagining of a beloved classic dessert, transforming traditional milk tart into a refined plated experience. The milk tart filling is smooth, creamy, and delicately spiced with warm cinnamon notes, then shaped into bite-sized pops with a silky texture and rich flavour profile. The pops are paired with chocolate soil — a crumbly, rich chocolate element that adds depth and contrast, resembling fine edible earth while delivering a balanced cocoa richness. The combination creates a contrast of textures: soft and creamy milk tart against the delicate crunch of the chocolate soil. Finished with artistic garnishes and fine-dining presentation, the dish offers a modern interpretation of comfort and nostalgia, turning a familiar favourite into a sophisticated dessert experience.

Entrée

Chicken lollipops with coconut peppadew sauce

Chicken lollipops with coconut peppadew sauce is a bold fusion starter that balances crisp, spicy comfort with creamy sweetness. The chicken is shaped into lollipops, marinated with aromatic spices, then deep-fried until golden and crunchy on the outside while staying juicy inside. It’s served with a smooth coconut-based sauce infused with peppadew peppers, giving it a unique contrast of gentle heat, tangy sweetness, and tropical richness. The sauce clings to the crispy chicken, creating a layered bite that moves from crunch to creaminess with a slow-building warmth. Finished with fresh herbs or microgreens, it’s the kind of dish that feels both street-inspired and fine-dining plated, familiar comfort elevated with refined flavour pairing.

Beef fillet with Potato Coulis and Onion Purée Quenelles, Dark Chocolate jus

Beef fillet with potato coulis, onion purée quenelles, and dark chocolate jus is a refined fine-dining plate that focuses on depth, balance, and contrast. The beef fillet is seared to perfection, creating a caramelised crust while keeping the centre tender, juicy, and pink. Its richness is the heart of the dish, elevated by a glossy dark chocolate jus that adds unexpected depth, slight bitterness, and a silky finish that enhances the natural beef flavour rather than overpowering it. Alongside, a smooth potato coulis provides a light, velvety base, softer and more fluid than traditional mash, offering comfort and subtle earthiness. The onion purée quenelles bring sweetness and complexity, carefully shaped into elegant spooned forms that melt into the plate. Together, the elements create a layered experience: savoury, slightly sweet, and deeply umami, with texture contrasts between silkiness and richness. It’s a modern gourmet interpretation of classic beef pairing, designed with precision plating and flavour harmony in mind.

Pistachio-coated Lamb chops with lantil Curry bedding

Pistachio-coated lamb chops on a lentil curry bedding is a refined fusion dish that brings together richness, texture, and deep aromatic warmth. The lamb chops are expertly seared to lock in their natural juiciness, then coated with a finely crushed pistachio crust that adds a nutty crunch and a subtle earthy sweetness. This crust enhances the lamb’s natural flavour without overpowering its tenderness. They are served atop a silky lentil curry bedding, slow-cooked lentils infused with warming spices, garlic, onion, and fragrant herbs. The curry is smooth yet hearty, acting as a luxurious base that absorbs the juices from the lamb. Each bite delivers contrast: the crisp pistachio crust, the tender lamb, and the creamy spiced lentils beneath. Finished with a light herb garnish or drizzle of reduced jus, it’s a dish that feels both comforting and elevated, rooted in tradition but presented with modern fine-dining elegance.
